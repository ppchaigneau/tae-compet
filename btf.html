Our Story

Eight Years of Excellence

The Bay Area Taekwondo Festival was founded in 2018 by a coalition of four Northern California dojangs united by a shared belief: that regional competition should prioritize sportsmanship, technical excellence, and community over trophies alone. The inaugural event, held at a gymnasium in Fremont, drew 134 competitors across six age and belt divisions.

By 2020 the festival had grown to six member schools and nearly 280 competitors, before the COVID-19 pandemic forced a two-year pause. When the BTF returned in 2022, it came back stronger — expanding to eight member schools and moving to its current home at Merrill F West High School in Tracy, where six competition rings can operate simultaneously.

Today the Bay Area Taekwondo Festival is one of the premier regional Taekwondo events in Northern California. The 2026 edition marks our eighth year and is expected to welcome over 450 competitors ranging from Tiny Tigers to Black Belt Masters, competing across Poomsae, Sparring, Board Breaking, and Speed Kicking.

Competition

Four Events

Competitors may enter one to four events. Registration fees are tiered — the more you enter, the better the value.

🥋
Poomsae (Form)
Competitors perform a WT-recognized poomsae from memory, judged by a panel of three on accuracy and presentation. Divided by age, belt division level, and gender.
🥊
Sparring
Continuous point sparring in a 2-minute round. Points awarded for legal kicks and punches to the trunk and head. Full protective gear required.
🪵
Board Breaking
Two attempts to break a set number of boards with a single technique. Judged on power, technique, and execution. Advanced divisions may add boards for difficulty points.
⚡
Speed Kicking
Maximum confirmed kicks to a held pad in a 10-second window. Kicks must meet technique standards verified by a floor judge. Score equals total valid kicks.

Rules & Judging

Judging Criteria

All events follow WT-aligned rules adapted for regional competition. Competitors should review the relevant criteria before the event day.

Belt Divisions

  • Novice (Lv. 0–2): White through early color belts
  • Intermediate (Lv. 3–5): Green through Blue rank belts
  • Advanced (Lv. 6–8): Red through pre-Black belts
  • Black Belt / Poom (Lv. 9): All Black Belts and Poom ranks compete together by age

Scoring (per judge, 3 judges)

CategoryMax Points
Accuracy — stances, techniques, direction, sequence6.0
Presentation — power, speed, balance, rhythm4.0
Total per judge10.0

Final score = average of 3 judges, displayed to one decimal place.

Deductions

  • −0.3 per momentary memory lapse (stopping <3 sec)
  • −0.5 for stopping (3+ seconds)
  • −1.0 per incorrect technique or sequence error
  • −1.0 for falling
  • −0.3 per step outside boundary
  • Incomplete poomsae results in disqualification

Competition Format

  • Competitor performs one WT-recognized poomsae per round
  • Division with 2–3 competitors: round-robin final
  • Division with 4+ competitors: preliminary → final rounds
  • Ties broken by highest Presentation score
  • Poomsae selection may be assigned by division or competitor's choice
  • Dobok (uniform) must be clean and properly worn

Scoring

TechniquePoints
Body kick (legal area)1
Head kick2
Spinning body kick2
Spinning head kick3
Punch to body1 (color belts only)

Match Format

  • Tiny Tiger / Youth: 1-minute continuous round
  • Junior and above: 2-minute continuous round
  • Winner by point superiority at time
  • KO or RSC (Referee Stop Contest) ends match immediately
  • 7-point gap triggers automatic win in color belt divisions
  • Tie after time: sudden-death golden point round (1 minute)

Required Equipment

  • WT-approved hogu (chest protector)
  • Head protector (helmet)
  • Forearm and shin/instep guards
  • Groin protector (male competitors)
  • Mouthguard — mandatory, no exceptions
  • Dobok — competitors may not wear jewelry or hard accessories

Penalties (Gam-jeom)

  • Each Gam-jeom = 1 point to opponent
  • Crossing the boundary line
  • Grabbing or holding opponent
  • Attacking a fallen opponent
  • Attacking prohibited areas (spine, back of head, groin)
  • Excessive contact to face with punch
  • Disrespectful behavior toward referee or opponent
  • 3 Gam-jeom = disqualification

Categories

  • Speed Break: One board, loosely held — technique and recoil judged
  • Power Break: Boards stacked — must break all cleanly in one strike
  • Competitors choose kick or hand technique (legal Taekwondo technique only)
  • Each competitor receives two attempts; best score counts

Board Counts by Division

DivisionBoards (Power)
Tiny Tiger / Youth1

Judging Criteria

  • Boards must break completely — cracks without full break do not count
  • Technique scored 1–10 on form, power, and execution
  • Bonus points available for additional boards or added height (Advanced/BB only)
  • Holder will re-set boards for second attempt if needed
  • Falling or losing balance after the break may incur a deduction

Safety Rules

  • Boards provided by tournament — competitors may not use their own
  • Board holders are trained volunteers — do not adjust holder position
  • Competitors must stop on judge's signal if safety concern arises
  • Injured competitors may withdraw before their second attempt
  • Competitors under 10 years old use padded practice boards only

Format

  • 10-second timed interval per competitor
  • Pad held at chest height of the competitor by a designated holder
  • Start signal given by floor judge; timer stops at 10 seconds
  • Score = total number of confirmed valid kicks
  • One warm-up kick allowed before the clock starts

Valid Kick Techniques

  • Front kick (ap chagi)
  • Roundhouse kick (dollyo chagi)
  • Side kick (yeop chagi)
  • Back kick (dwitchagi) — Advanced and Black Belt only
  • Each kick must make audible contact with the pad
  • Judge confirms each kick with a point counter in real-time

Alternating Foot Rule

  • Novice & Intermediate: Single-foot kicks allowed (same foot, repeated)
  • Advanced & Black Belt: Must alternate feet — L, R, L, R sequence
  • Two consecutive kicks with the same foot in Advanced/BB division are not counted
  • Judge signals invalid kick immediately; competitor continues

Disqualification Conditions

  • Grabbing or pushing the holder or pad
  • Starting before the judge's signal
  • Continuing to kick after the stop signal
  • Unsportsmanlike conduct during the round
  • No re-dos unless judge error is confirmed within 30 seconds

Check-In Requirements

  • All competitors must check in by 8:30 AM — no exceptions
  • Valid school membership required; belt certificate may be requested
  • Competitors must wear their dobok during all events
  • Minors must be accompanied by a parent or guardian at check-in
  • Entry forms and payment must be completed prior to event day

Coaches & Corners

  • Maximum one coach per competitor on the floor
  • Coaches must remain in designated corner areas
  • Coaching from outside the designated area results in a warning
  • Coaches must be respectful to referees, judges, and opposing competitors at all times
  • Schools are responsible for the conduct of their coaches and spectators

Protests & Appeals

  • Protests must be filed within 2 minutes of a decision
  • A $20 protest fee is required at filing
  • Fee is refunded if the protest is upheld
  • Protest reviewed by the Head Referee and Tournament Director
  • Tournament Director's ruling is final

Sportsmanship

  • All competitors must bow to opponents and judges before and after each match
  • Disrespectful behavior results in immediate disqualification from that event
  • Deliberate injury to an opponent results in disqualification from the entire tournament
  • Spectators engaging in disruptive behavior may be asked to leave
  • The BTF is a drug-free and alcohol-free event
