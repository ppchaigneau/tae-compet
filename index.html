About the Tournament

The Dragon Cup is an open international Taekwondo competition welcoming athletes of all belt levels, from white belt beginners to black belt masters. Sanctioned by the World Taekwondo Federation, this two-day event features Kyorugi (sparring) and Poomsae (forms) divisions across all age groups.
